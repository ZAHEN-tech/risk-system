Data pipeline

Attendance, coursework and assessment data normally flow in from the student information system and LMS on a schedule (e.g. nightly). In this prototype you can seed a synthetic dataset and trigger the risk engine manually.

How risk is scored

risk_score = 0.35 × (100 − attendance%) + 0.35 × (100 − assessment avg) + 0.20 × (100 − submission rate) + 0.10 × trend penalty.
Bands: ≥60 High · 30–59 Medium · <30 Low. Weights are configurable in risk_engine.py and should be recalibrated against an institution's own historical outcome data over time.

A logistic-regression model trained on historical outcomes runs alongside this rule in shadow mode, scoring every enrollment for comparison without yet driving alerts on its own.
